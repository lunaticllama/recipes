    Directions

    1. Rub the Pulled Pork Dry Rub: Mix all the dry rub ingredients and rub it all over the pork shoulder picnic pieces. Then, put the pork shoulder picnic in the fridge for 30 minutes to overnight.

    2. Brown the Pork Shoulder (See Tips): Add 1 tablespoon of oil into cast iron pan. Ensure to coat the oil over the whole bottom of the pan. Add the pork shoulder pieces into the pot. Brown the pork shoulder on all sides. Remove and set aside. Add onion and scap to pick up browned bits until onion is softened - approximately 5 minutes. Ad ½ cup of water to deglaze.

    3. Put the onion and water in pressure cooker. Add half of the BBQ Sauce and stir to mix together. Add in sprigs of thyme and oregano from garden, if available.

    4. Put browned pork pieces into the pressure cooker. Make sure the skin side of the pork is facing up. Close lid and pressure cook at High Pressure for 75 minutes if bone-in. Turn off the heat and fully Natural Release (roughly 15 minutes).

    5. Fork Tender Check: Open the lid carefully. Take one piece of pork shoulder out and see if you can shred through the meat easily with two forks. If it is not fork-tender, cook for an additional 10 to 20 minutes at High Pressure, fully Natural Release.

    6. Shred the Pork & De-Fat the Sauce: Use some cool Pulled Pork Shredder Claws or two regular forks to shred the pork shoulder meat. Use a fat separator to separate the fat from the BBQ sauce.

    7. Season, Reduce, Serve: Add in the reserved half of the uncooked BBQ sauce to the sauce from the fat separator. Reduce the BBQ sauce to your desired thickness. Taste the BBQ sauce and adjust seasonings. Place the pulled pork back into the BBQ sauce. Mix well and serve!

      Serve with reserved BBQ sauce.
