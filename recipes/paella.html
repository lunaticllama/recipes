    Paella

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. In paella pan over a stove at high heat, brown chicken, skin-side down, first, then flip and add chorizo and continue browning meat. Once thoroughly browned, add green beans and artichoke hearts. Top with rosemary sprigs. When vegetables are cooked softened, add salmorra as desired. Add saffron and then rice, and evenly distribute with meat and vegetables. Add stock to top of paella.

    2. Top cooked paella with parsley and lemons.

    Salmorra

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Saute in ¼ of olive oil peppers and garlic for 1 minute. Then add tomatoes and cook until thickened, about 15 or 20 minutes. Deglaze with a little white wine or sherry and then add paprike and continue cooking until desired thickness.
