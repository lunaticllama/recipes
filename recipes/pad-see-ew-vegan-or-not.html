    Pad See Ew (Vegan or not)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Mix first five ingredients together for sauce and set aside. Prepare rice noodles to stir fry according to package directions, typically you will have to run them over cold water to stop the cooking process, but you should do that regardless of what the package directions say.

    2. Add 1 tbsp of oil to large cast iron skillet, heat over medium-high heat, and add in broccoli. Stir occasionally, but deeply char on as many sides as possible, about 10 minutes. Add in some water (stand back, because it will immediately steam) and cook until no water is left and broccoli is completely cooked through. Finish broccoli with 2 tbsp of sauce and mix to incorporate, set aside broccoli in a bowl, and place skillet back on medium-high heat.

    3. Add 1 tbsp of oil and add in mushrooms (or chicken). Stir-fry until nicely browned and soft (or cooked through), about 5 to 7 minutes. Finish with garlic slices and 2 tbsp of sauce, cooking another 2 or 3 minutes. Set aside mushrooms (or chicken) and garlic to the bowl with broccoli.

    4. Add 1 tbsp of oil and stir fry bok choy, first only with the stems. Once the stems have cooked 5 minutes, add leaves and 1 tbsp of sauce and stir fry until wilted. Move bok choy to edge of pan and add 1 tbsp of oil to open area of pan. Add eggs and stir fry until almost cooked through, breaking egg into smaller pieces. Set aside bok choy and egg mixture to the bowl with the other vegetables.

    5. Add 1 tbsp of oil and add noodles. Stir fry for 2 minutes and then add 2 tbsp of sauce. Once noodles are evenly coated with sauce, add in rest of vegetables (and chicken), and any remaining sauce, and cook until everything is heat through.

    6. Serve with cilantro and siracha.
