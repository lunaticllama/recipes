    Spicy Sesame Squash

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Pre-heat oven to 425F. Thoroughly mix together oils, gochujang, and soy sauce. Line a baking sheet with foil.

    2. Toss squash in a bowl or zip-lock bag with the gochujang sauce until thoroughly coated. Place on baking sheet and cover each piece with a generous amount of sesame seeds. Place in oven.

    3. Roast, rotating sheets once, until tender and browned on some edges, 25–30 minutes.

    4. Place in serving bowl and top with scallions.
