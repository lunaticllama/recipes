    Greek Lamb Stew/Pasta Sauce with Orzo

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Pat meat dry using paper towels, then season generously using salt and pepper. Heat olive oil over medium-high heat in a large Dutch oven. Add meat and sear until browned on all sides, 6 to 8 minutes total. Transfer browned meat to a plate using a slotted spoon.

    2. Turn heat to medium. Toss onions into pot, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom, and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 more minute until fragrant.

    3. Return meat and any juices to Dutch oven. Pour in wine, scraping up frond, and simmer until reduced by half. Add oregano, paprika, cinnamon, nutmeg, and bay leaf. Add tomatoes with juices, crushing them slightly with wooden spoon.

    4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and gently simmer 45 minutes to 1 hour, until meat is tender.

    5. Stir in orzo pasta directly into stew. Add a splash of water or broth if liquid level is too low. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, about 15 minutes until orzo is tender and sauce is absorbed.

    6. Remove from heat and let sit 5 to 10 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley and feta cheese before serving.

    Alternative

    Directions

    1. Substitute ground lamb, reduce simmering time to 10 minutes, cook orzo separately, and serve as a pasta sauce.
