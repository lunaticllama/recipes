    Cheddar Biscuits (Like Red Lobster)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C).

    2. Place the flour, baking powder, baking soda, garlic powder, and salt together in a large bowl or in a large food processor and whisk or pulse until combined. Add the cubed butter and cut into the dry ingredients with a pastry cutter or by pulsing several times in the processor until coarse crumbs form. (If you used a food processor, pour the mixture into a large bowl.) Fold in the shredded cheese.

    3. Make a well in the center of the mixture. Pour 1 cup (240ml) buttermilk and drizzle honey on top. Fold everything together with a large spoon or spatula until it begins to come together—do not overwork the dough (it will be shaggy and crumbly with some wet spots).

    4. Pour the dough and any dough crumbles onto a floured work surface and gently bring together with generously floured hands. Flatten into a 3/4-inch-thick rectangle, then fold one end in toward the center and the other end on top of that (like a business letter). Flatten back into a 3/4-inch-thick rectangle and repeat the folding and flattening process once more.

    5. Flatten into a final 3/4-inch-thick rectangle and cut into 2.5- or 3-inch circles with a biscuit cutter, pressing straight down without twisting. Re-roll scraps until all the dough is used.

    6. Arrange biscuits close together (touching) in a 10-inch cast iron skillet or on a parchment paper-lined baking sheet. Brush the tops with the remaining 2 tablespoons buttermilk.

    7. Bake for 18–22 minutes or until tops are golden brown. Remove from the oven. Mix the 2 tablespoons of butter, garlic powder, and parsley together, generously brush on the warm biscuits, and serve warm.
