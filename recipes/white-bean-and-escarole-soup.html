    White Bean and Escarole Soup

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Saute onion in olive oil over medium heat until translucent (about 5 minutes). Add escarole and wilt. Once the escarole has wilted, add garlic and saute another minute. Then add stock and bring up to simmer over medium heat.

    2. Add white beans and any herbs and simmer until beans are cooked through and tender about 15 minutes. Once off heat, add in cheese if desired and stir to evenly incorporate. Serve warm, with a baguette for dipping if desired.
