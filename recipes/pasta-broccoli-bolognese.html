    Pasta Broccoli Bolognese

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and salt. Cook broccoli in salted boiling water until crisp-tender, about 3 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer broccoli to a colander and let cool (save pot of water for cooking pasta).

    2. Heat 2 Tbsp. oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook sausage and a generous pinch of red pepper flakes, breaking up meat into smaller pieces with a wooden spoon and stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through, 6–8 minutes.

    3. Salt water, and cook pasta until tender but not mushy. Reserve about a cup of water before draining pasta.

    4. While pasta is cooking, add garlic, shallot, sundried tomatoes, and blanched broccoli to pot with sausage, and cook for several minutes until fragrant. Break apart broccoli and sausage with the back of a wooden spoon or potato masher while everything cooks. Deglaze pan with white wine and simmer until wine is reduced. Add cream and cheese and incorporate, but do not cook cream for more than 1 minute before adding pasta.

    5. When pasta is done, drain and toss sausage and broccoli sauce and add at least ½ cup of reserved pasta cooking water. Toss everything together as pasta finishes cooking. If the dressed pasta is dry, add a little reserved cooking water. Add plenty of black pepper and more cheese to taste, and serve.
