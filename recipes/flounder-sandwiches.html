    Flounder Sandwiches

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Combine the mayonnaise and sriracha. Stir well and set aside.

    2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the vinegar, sugar, and salt. Add the red onions and let hang out for 20 minutes or so.

    3. In a third bowl, add the fish sauce and the flounder. Toss well, cover with plastic and let hang out in the fridge for 20 minutes. Dry on paper towels.

    4. Place a large skillet set over high heat. When hot, pour in a tablespoon of canola oil and then add the flounder, one piece at a time if pan would be crowded with all of the pieces. Sear on both sides. Repeat if necessary with the rest of the fillets.

    5. Toast the bread/rolls if desired. Then smear the sriracha mayonnaise on the insides of the cut bread. Add the fish, herbs and/or greens, and pickled onions between the bread. Serve.
