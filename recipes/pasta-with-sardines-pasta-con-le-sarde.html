    Pasta with Sardines [Pasta con le Sarde]

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Put a pot of water on high heat to boil for pasta. When water is boiling, salt water (it should be almost as salty as the ocean), and put pasta in to cook.

    2. In a high-sided saute or dutch oven (pasta will finish in sauce), saute over medium-low heat in 1 Tbs of olive oil onion and fennel, if using, until onion is translucent and vegetables soft. Add fennel seeds, pepper flakes, and capers and saute another minute. Add sardines, while avoid adding too much oil from tin. Saute sardines until warmed through, breaking them up into small pieces with the back of a wooden spoon. You may have to take this pan off heat at this point if the pasta is not done.

    3. When pasta is almost done, drain, but reserve at least ½ cup of the pasta cooking water. With the sardine mixture over low heat, add pasta directly to pan with reserve cooking water. If using any greens, you should add them at this point as well. Mix pasta and sauce until thoroughly combined and the water is absorbed by the pasta.

    4. Add breadcrumbs and fresh herbs to pasta and toss lightly. Serve immediately.
