    Phil’s Chinese Cabbage

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Place a large saute pan (preferably cast-iron) on medium-high heat and add 2 tablespoons of the oil. Quarter cabbage and then cut across the sides of the cabbage, creating thin ribbons. Once oil is smoking, add in cabbage. The cabbage should be able to spread in an relatively thin, even layer across the pan. Do not stir the cabbage for several minutes, until one side is browned. Begin stirring every minute or so, sometimes giving a side a break, until the cabbage is browned on all sides and it is beginning to lose its moisture.

    2. Add a tablespoon or two of water to the pan and the smashed cloves of garlic. Cook cabbage until water has evaporated and the cabbage is cooked to your taste. If you want the cabbage softer, add another tablespoon or two after the initial water boils off.

    3. Before the last addition of water has completely reduced, add any combination of soy sauces, oyster sauce, vinegar, and chili oil or chili crisp. Toss the cabbage in the pan to spread the sauces evenly over the cabbage.
