    Chicken Wings (Oven or Broiler)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Line rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil, and set rack inside. Carefully dry chicken wings with paper towels. Place wings in large bowl, sprinkle with baking powder and salt and pepper, and toss until thoroughly and evenly coated. Place on rack, leaving slight space between each wing.

    2. Place baking sheet with wings in refrigerator and allow to rest, uncovered, at least 8 hours, and up to 18 hours.

    3. Adjust oven rack to upper-middle position and preheat oven to 450°F or pre-heat broiler if possible. Add chicken wings to oven or place 1-2 inches below broiler and cook for 20 minutes. Flip wings and continue to cook until crisp and golden brown, 15 to 25 minutes longer.

    4. Toss with sauce as desired and serve.
