    Tofu Stir Fry

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat oven to 400F. Take tofu out of package and place on top of 2 paper towels on a plate. Cover with 2 more paper towels and place a heavy pan on top (cast iron works well). Let it the pan press the tofu for 10 minutes, changing the paper towels if desired once fully soaked.

    2. Slice tofu in desire pieces. Place on a sheet pan lined with parchment or Silpat. Bake in oven for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through, until brown all over.

    3. While tofu is baking, heat oil in wok or pan. When hot, place vegetables in pan. If using more than 1 type of vegetable, stir fry each vegetable separately, adding more oil as needed. Always stir fry leafy greens first.

    4. Before adding baked tofu to pan, add scallions, chilis, or any other small additions to the pan. Let stir fry for 45 seconds, then add baked tofu to pan. Then add sauce to pan and stir continuously for several minutes, until everything is heated through, and the sauce coats the vegetables and tofu.
