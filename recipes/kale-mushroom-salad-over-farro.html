    Kale Mushroom Salad Over Farro

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Start cooking farro per instructions, substituting stock for water.

    2. If using, fry the bacon until slightly crisp. Drain on a paper towel. Reserve bacon grease for another use (roasting potatoes, cooking greens, etc.)

    3. Add the butter to a skillet. Add the onions and cook them over medium-low heat for 10 to 15 minutes, until soft. Remove them to a plate. Add the mushrooms, stir, then add the wine, and season with salt and pepper. Turn the heat to medium-high and cook the mushrooms for several minutes, until most of the wine has evaporated and the mushrooms are soft. Remove them from the heat and set them aside.

    4. Whisk the olive oil, vinegar, thyme, salt, and pepper to form a vinaigrette.

    5. Toss kale in a large bowl with half the dressing. Then add mushrooms, onions, and bacon and toss again. Finally, add the goat cheese and more dressing if needed, and toss. (Reserve extra dressing for another use.)

    6. Serve kale and mushroom salad over the farro.
