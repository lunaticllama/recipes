    Braised Beef Tacos (Beef Barbacoa)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat a braising pot over medium heat and add olive oil. Season the beef with salt and pepper generously, and brown all around to sear in a pot. Remove the beef and set aside.

      Add the onion, pepper, and saute for 5 minutes. Add garlic into the pot and saute for 2 more minutes. Splash with red wine or brandy and let it reduce.

    2. Return the beef to the pot. Add the diced tomatoes, chili in adobo sauce (full or half-can), chili powder, cayenne pepper, bay leaf, and cilantro. Drizzle water over and cover. Simmer over low heat for 3 hours or braise in oven at 350F for 3 hours. Using a full can of chili in adobo sauce will produce a spicy taco with a strong chipotle taste. Use less as need to your taste.

    3. Remove the beef to a cutting board and shred with forks. Place the shredded beef into a serving bowl and add some gravy left in a pot to the beef, mix well. Season with salt and pepper if needed.

    4. Serve with tortilla and other toppings like guacamole, lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, etc.
