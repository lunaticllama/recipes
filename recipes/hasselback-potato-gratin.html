    Hasselback Potato Gratin

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 400°F (200°C). Grease a 2-quart casserole dish (or 9x13-inch baking dish) with the softened butter.

    2. Combine the grated Gruyère and Parmigiano-Reggiano in a large bowl. Transfer ⅓ of the cheese mixture to a separate bowl and set aside for the final topping.

    3. Add the heavy cream, minced garlic, and chopped thyme to the large bowl with the remaining cheese. Season generously with kosher salt and black pepper.

    4. Add the sliced potatoes to the bowl and toss with your hands until every slice is coated with the cream mixture, making sure to separate any slices that are sticking together so the liquid gets in between them.

    5. Pick up a handful of potatoes, organize them into a neat stack, and lay them in the prepared casserole dish with their edges aligned vertically. Continue placing potatoes in the dish, working around the perimeter and into the center until all the potatoes are added and tightly packed. If necessary, slice an additional potato, coat it with the cream mixture, and add it to the dish.

    6. Pour any excess cream and cheese mixture evenly over the potatoes until it comes about halfway up the sides of the dish.

    7. Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake for 30 minutes.

    8. Remove the foil and continue baking until the top is pale golden brown, about 30 minutes longer.

    9. Carefully remove from the oven, sprinkle the reserved ⅓ of the cheese mixture evenly over the top, and return to the oven.

    10. Bake until deep golden brown and crisp on top, about 20 to 30 minutes longer. Remove from the oven, let rest for a few minutes, and serve.
