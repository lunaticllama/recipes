    Korean Spicy Beef Soup

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Cut brisket into 1 inch cubes and season liberally with salt (~1-2 teaspoons). In pressure cooker, put on saute function and add onion. Saute until soft and golden, approximately 5-10 minutes. Add garlic and ginger and stir for 1 minute. Add brisket cubes and seasonings - the gochujang, soy sauce, Better than Bouillon beef stock, hondashi, cinnamon, star anise, wine, rice vinegar, sugar. Add 8 cups of water or stock. If using beef stock, omit the Better than Bouillon beef stock.

    2. Seal cooker and cook for 45 minutes at 12.0 PSI. Allow for natural pressure release for 20 minutes and then manually release the rest of the pressure. Set to simmer/reduce function and add bok choy and mushrooms and cook for 7-8 minutes or until bok choy is completely wilted.

    3. Serve with noodles, if desired. I like to add chinese chili oil (specifically chili oil with fermented black beans) to up the heat and funk.
