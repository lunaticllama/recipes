    Kale, Poblano, and Black Bean Tacos

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. If using sausage, brown in olive oil over medium heat, using back of wooden spoon to break up into small pieces. Otherwise, saute onion and peppers over medium heat.

    2. Once onions are translucent and peppers are soft, add garlic and kale and ¼ cup of water. Cover if possible and saute/steam kale until tender.

    3. Add black beans, chili powder, salt and pepper, and sausage if using to kale mixture. Saute until beans are cooked through and flavors are incorporated. Add water 1 Tbs at time if mixture is too dry and burning. Serve in soft tacos with hot sauce, avocados, cheese of choice, radishes, etc.
