    Thai “Puffy” Eggs

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Combine eggs, lime juice or vinegar, fish sauce, water, and cornstarch in a medium bowl. Beat with a fork until frothy. If the flour forms a few lumps, break up as many as possible with your fingers.

    2. Heat the vegetable oil in a small pot or a round-bottom wok set over medium-high heat until lightly smoking and pour the egg mixture into the oil in one go.

    3. The egg mixture will immediately puff up. Do not disturb it, but shake the pan to make sure the eggs loosen and do not stick to the pan. After 30 to 40 seconds, flip the omelet. There’s no need to keep it nice and round; we want asymmetrical edges. Let the other side cook for another 30 to 40 seconds. Remove the omelet from the pan and serve immediately.
