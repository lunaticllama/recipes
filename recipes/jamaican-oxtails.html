    Jamaican Oxtails

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Season oxtails with jerk seasoning if using and otherwise with salt and pepper and brown in neutral oil in dutch oven.

    2. Reserve oxtails and saute all vegetables except garlic, ginger, and hot pepper. Once the onion has softened, add garlic, ginger, and hot pepper, saute 1 minute and then add tomato paste.

    3. Once tomato paste and all vegetables are aromatic, add in broth and then the rest of the seasonings. Once mixed, add in oxtails and braise for 2 to 3 hours or until tender, adding more broth if the liquid evaporates or is too thick. Traditionally butter beans are added but I have never done so and serve it over coconut rice and sauteed greens, preferably collared greens or callaloo (which is very hard to source outside of Jamaica and the Cayman Islands.)
