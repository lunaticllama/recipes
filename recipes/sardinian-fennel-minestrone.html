    Sardinian Fennel Minestrone

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. If using dried beans: Soak beans at least 6 hours, or overnight; drain and rinse.

    2. Peel the fava beans by squeezing each one between your thumb and other fingers. The skins should slip off pretty easily. In a soup pot, simmer beans in water to cover for 45 minutes to 1 hour, adding lentils after 30 minutes. Drain beans and lentils.

    3. If using canned beans: Rinse and simmer beans and lentils in water to cover for 30 minutes, then drain.

    4. For the minestrone: In large soup pot, combine beans with all vegetables in water and bring to a boil. Lower heat to medium-low and simmer for 15 minutes. Add fregula and simmer for another 15 minutes.

    5. Note: We just use what canned beans we want, fregula is a Sardinian semolina pasta in pastini shape - we can use anything. The idea is a soup made with fennel, swiss chard, 1 tomato, vegetables, beans, and small pasta. This is a soup appropriate for any season, including summer.
