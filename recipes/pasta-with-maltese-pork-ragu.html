    Pasta with Maltese Pork Ragu

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Brown pork shoulder in dutch oven, remove, and then pancetta, remove. Preheat oven to 325.

    2. Saute all vegetables, adding garlic and fennel seed at end. Then add tomato paste, holding back if a full tin looks too much, and saute more, adding back pancetta. Add wine, stock, thyme, and rosemary and then the pork shoulder whole. The liquid should go about halfway up the pork.

    3. Brown pork in oven for about 3 hours, adding in more wine or water if liquid levels reduce too much.

    4. Once pork is easily shredded with a fork, remove, and shred, discarding any bones. In the meantime, reduce the sauce if necessary.

    5. Add shredded pork back to sauce with cream, mustard, and parmigiano. Add 1 tsp of orange zest. Orange is very strong so use less at first to not overpower the dish. Cook another 15-30 minutes to bring the flavors together. This is best saved and reheated the following day for even better flavor. Serve with pasta with basil.
