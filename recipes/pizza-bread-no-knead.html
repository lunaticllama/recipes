    Pizza Bread (No-knead)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, yeast, salt, sugar, oregano, basil, garlic powder, and crushed red pepper (if using). Using a silicone spatula or wooden spoon, mix in the chopped add-ins (pepperoni, sun-dried tomatoes, olives) and mozzarella cheese. Pour in the water and gently mix together until all the flour is moistened (use your hands if needed; the dough will be sticky).

    2. Cover the bowl tightly with plastic wrap or aluminum foil and set on the counter at room temperature. Allow to rise for 3-6 hours until initial proof looks complete. Then, place the covered dough in the refrigerator for at least 12 hours and up to 3 days.

    3. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and, using generously floured hands, shape into a ball. Transfer the dough to a large piece of parchment paper, place inside a medium-size mixing bowl or cutting board,, and let rest for 1 hour.

    4. Preheat the oven to 450°F (232°C) 1 hour with a dutch oven inside.

    5. Bake in the preheated Dutch oven or via your preferred method until crusty and golden brown for 30 minutes, covered. Uncover and bake another 10 minutes or until crust has desired browning.
