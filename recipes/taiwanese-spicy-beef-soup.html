    Taiwanese Spicy Beef Soup

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Boil enough water in a large pot/wok or dutch oven to cover the shanks, add the shanks and simmer for 10 minutes. Remove the shanks and clean under running water. Drain the remaining water/thin broth through a sieve into a large enough bowl to hold the liquid.

    2. Heat the oil on medium heat in the large pot/wok. Add onion, ginger, garlic, scallions and fry for several minutes. Add tomato and saute another couple of minutes. Then add tomato paste, broad bean paste, soy sauces, and cooking wine.

    3. Add back in reserved broth/water and the shanks. Add in all of the spices or make a spice sachet for them and then add. Cook the soup on a low simmer for 3-4 hours.

    4. When beef is tender, remove shanks and tear meat into bite size pieces. Strain soup through a sieve, preferably with cheese cloth covering the sieve.

    5. Prepare a bowl with a serving of freshly boiled Asian noodles of choice and bok choy boiled in noodle water. Add pieces of beef and pour in broth to desired amount. Add chopped scallions, cilantro, chili oil, and/or spicy Sichuan pickled vegetables such as mustard stems.
