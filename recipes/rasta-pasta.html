    Rasta Pasta

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Season chicken/shrimp with half of jerk seasoning and then saute in dutch oven or saute pan in olive oil for several minutes until browned.

    2. Remove chicken/shrimp and add all of the vegetables but the garlic and scotch bonnet. Add more oil if necessary and saute until soft. Begin cooking pasta

    3. Add garlic, scotch bonnet, and reserved jerk seasoning as desired. Season with salt and pepper. Add chicken/shrimp back and in and add dairy and bring to a low simmer.

    4. When pasta is finished add to sauce with a ½ cup of pasta cooking liquid. Serve with chopped scallions and/or cilantro.
