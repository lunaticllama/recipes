    Pumpkin Ravioli

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Combine roasted squash, garlic, cheese and herbs.

    2. Roll out fresh pasta dough into sheets.

    3. Spoon approximately a tablespoon of the squash mixture in even spacing along a sheet of pasta. Either make several rows and top with another sheet of pasta or fold single sheet and then use pasta cutter to cut into ravioli. Repeat for all pasta sheets. Having extra squash mixture is not unusual.
