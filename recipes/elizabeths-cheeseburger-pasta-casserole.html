    Elizabeth’s Cheeseburger Pasta Casserole

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Brown the ground beef: Heat a large 12-inch skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef, season with salt and pepper, and brown, breaking into smaller pieces with a spatula or spoon. When browned, place onto a paper towel lined plate, and drain the beef. Set aside. (You can add ½ tsp of dry mustard to beef as it cooks as well.)

    2. Cook the pepper, mushrooms and onion: In the same pan, add in the bell pepper and onion. After vegetables soften, add mushroom slices. Cook until all is golden brown and softened and mushrooms have released their liquid. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Season with salt and pepper. Transfer this mixture to another plate and set aside.

    3. Make the sauce: In a saucepan, add butter and melt. Add flour and cook 1-2 minutes, until flour is golden. Add half-and-half and whisk until the sauce thickens. Add stock and whisk until the sauce thickens.

    4. Cook the noodles: Add the egg noodles and cook according to instructions.

    5. Assemble and broil casserole: Turn oven onto broiler setting. In a casserole dish, add the ground beef, green peppers, mushrooms, and onions, egg noodles, and sauce. Stir to combine. Lastly, stir in the shredded cheese, or simply put cheese on top of casserole. Place under broiler and broiler 2-3 minutes until cheese is brown and bubbly. Watch carefully as it broils so that it doesn’t overcook. Serve immediately.
