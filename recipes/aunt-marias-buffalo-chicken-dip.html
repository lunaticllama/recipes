    Aunt Maria’s Buffalo Chicken Dip

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 350F.

    2. Mix the cream cheese, blue cheese dressing and Frank’s hot sauce in a bowl until well combined. Add in chopped chicken and put in casserole or other oven proof bowl. Add blue cheese and scallions to top, if using.

    3. Bake in oven until ingredients and cheese have melted and chicken is cooked through. Serve warm with chips and celery.
