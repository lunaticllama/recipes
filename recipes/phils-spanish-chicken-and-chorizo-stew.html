    Phil’s Spanish Chicken and Chorizo Stew

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat a braising pot over medium heat and add olive oil. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Brown the chorizo, and then reserve to a plate and then brown chicken starting with skin side first. Remove the chicken and reserve with chorizo.

    2. Drain pot of fat, then add back another two tablespoon of fat and the onion and pepper. Saute for 10 minutes. Add garlic saute for 2 more minutes. Add in the tomatoes, stir to get up any browned bits. Then add in chickpeas, olives, and white wine. Season with salt and pepper.

    3. Once wine is reduced slightly and alcohol is cooked off, add soy sauce, herbs, chorizo, and chicken, nestling the chicken on top of everything else. Bring back to a simmer.

    4. Cover and braise in the oven at 350F for 30 minutes. Uncover for next 15 minutes until liquid is reduced to desired level. Remove from oven and remove chicken thighs from pot. Mix in spinach into stew and then nestle chicken thighs back into stew. Cover and cook another 5 or 7 minutes until the spinach is wilted.
