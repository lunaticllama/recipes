    Cabbage Slaw (For Fritters, Pancakes and Tacos)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. In a salad bowl combine all of the ingredients besides the cabbage and mix until evenly combined. Add cabbage and thoroughly coat. Serve slaw on top of zucchini, potato, or other fritters, pancakes, or tacos.

    Simple version

    Directions

    1. Toss cabbage with ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp sugar, lime juice, and apple cider vinegar. Let it sit for half an hour in the fridge.
