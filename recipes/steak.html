    Steak

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Thoroughly dry steaks with paper towels. You will probably need two paper towels per steak and aim to get as much moisture as possible. You can do this ahead of time and then store uncovered in a refrigerator to further dry for several hours before cooking. Either way, allow steaks to sit covered in the kitchen at least 30 minutes prior to cooking to bring the steaks to room temperature.

    2. Heat a cast iron pan until very hot. Pour in a generous tablespoon or two of oil, depending on size of pan. The oil should start smoking.

    3. Season steaks with salt and pepper and carefully slide into hot pan with oil.

    4. Cook, 6-8 minutes in total, flipping the steaks often. If you have an instant read thermometer, you want a consistent internal temperature of 125F to 130F for medium. In the last minute, add the butter to the pan (and the herbs if using), and coat the steaks in the butter.

    5. Let the steaks rest covered for 10 minutes, and then serve.

    Asian Marinade

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. In step one above, after thoroughly drying the steaks, add them to a large, plastic zip-lock bag. Mix all of the marinade ingredients together and cover the steak. Allow them to sit overnight to 2 hours prior to cooking in a refrigerator. Make sure to take out the bag ahead of time to allow steaks to come up to room temperature. Make sure no garlic or ginger pieces are sticking to the steak and pat dry with paper towels before cooking steaks (they do not have to be completely dry, but there should not be excessive marinade sticking to the steaks.) You should plan on seasoning with much less salt (due to the soy sauce), and you can omit the black pepper if you want.
