    Stifado

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Pat the meat dry and season well with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil over high heat in a Dutch oven or heavy-bottom pot. Working in batches to avoid overcrowding, sear the meat until lightly browned on all sides (about 1 to 2 minutes per side). Transfer the browned pieces to a bowl, cover, and set aside.

    2. Reduce the heat to medium and add the peeled pearl onions. Cook until softened but not caramelized, about 10 minutes. Add the chopped garlic and cook for 1 more minute.

    3. Stir in the tomato paste, bay leaves, cinnamon, ground clove, and allspice berries. Let the mixture caramelize for 1 minute, then return the meat and any accumulated juices to the pot, stirring to coat.

    4. Pour in the red wine and cognac, using a wooden spoon to scrape up the fond from the bottom of the pot. Simmer for 5 minutes to let the alcohol evaporate slightly.

    5. Add the tomatoes and enough warm water to cover the meat. Reduce the heat to a medium-low slow simmer, cover, and cook until the meat is tender and the sauce has thickened (about 1 hour 40 minutes to 2 hours). Stir halfway through, checking to ensure there is enough liquid and adding a bit of hot water if needed.

    6. Adjust seasoning to taste. Remove the bay leaves, cinnamon stick, and allspice berries. Let the stew sit uncovered for 15 minutes before serving warm over orzo, rice, mashed potatoes, or with crusty bread.
