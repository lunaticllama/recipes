    Leek Gratin

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Halve leeks lengthwise, leaving halves attached at root (or cut right at root end), and rinse very, very well, shaking off water.

    2. In a large skillet, combine cream and leeks; season with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat; reduce to a medium simmer, cover, and cook 5 minutes. Uncover and simmer until leeks are tender, 15 minutes.

    3. Transfer leeks and sauce to a shallow 2-quart baking dish. Sprinkle with cheese and bake until golden and bubbling, about 35 minutes. Let gratin rest 10 minutes before serving.
