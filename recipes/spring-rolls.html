    Spring Rolls

    Ingredients

    Peanut Sauce Ingredients

    Fried Tofu Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Combine peanut sauce ingredients in small and whisk together. Whisk in small amounts of oil until if it has consistency and desired flavor. I usually add 2 to 4 tablespoons.

    2. Prep all of the vegetables. You can add or subtract depending on what you have on hand or want in the spring rolls.

    3. Prepare tofu. First, line a plate with paper towels and place block of tofu on it. Cover with paper towels and press the tofu with a cast iron pan or other heavy object for 10 minutes. Chop pressed tofu into slices or cubes.

    4. Place 3 tablespoons of cornstarch in a large ziplock bag with 1 tablespoon of soy sauce. Add approximately ¼ cup of water or so until a loose batter forms. Add tofu and toss until all tofu pieces are evenly covered by cornstarch batter. Add more cornstarch if necessary to make batter stick to tofu.

    5. Heat up peanut or safflower oil until very hot. Add tofu and fry on all sides until golden. You will probably have to do this in two batches. Drain tofu on paper towel-lined plate. When all tofu has been fried, toss fried tofu with 1 tablespoon of soy sauce and sesame oil.

    6. At dining table have 1 bowl with water to dunk dried spring roll wrappers in. One at time dunk in water until pliable. Then place soaked wrapper on plate and add ingredients. I start with any bigger leafier greens to help hold everything together, then the peanut sauce, then vegetables, then tofu, and, finally, the cilantro and hot pepper slices. Roll spring close and eat. Repeat. Normal dinner is usually 3-4 spring rolls per person.
