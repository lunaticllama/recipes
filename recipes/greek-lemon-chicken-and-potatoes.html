    Greek Lemon Chicken and Potatoes

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Add garlic, olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, honey, dried oregano, dried thyme, sea salt, and black pepper to a blender and blend until smooth. Place chicken thighs in a sealable bag or large bowl, pour the marinade over, and mix to coat thoroughly. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

    2. Preheat oven to 425°F with the rack set to the middle level. Arrange the dried potato chunks in a single layer in a baking dish and pour half of the used marinade over them, using your hands to coat them thoroughly.

    3. Place the chicken thighs on top of the potatoes, skin side up, and drizzle the remaining marinade over the chicken.

    4. Bake for 10 minutes, then reduce the oven temperature to 375°F. Continue baking for 45 to 55 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 185°F. If the potatoes need more time to become tender and brown, transfer the chicken to a plate, tent with foil, raise the oven temperature back to 425°F, and roast the potatoes for a few extra minutes.

    5. Let the dish sit lightly tented with foil for 10 to 15 minutes before serving to reabsorb the pan juices. Garnish with fresh oregano and lemon slices if desired.
