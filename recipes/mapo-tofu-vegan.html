    Mapo Tofu (Vegan)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. In a wok or large pot such as a dutch oven, gently fry the mushrooms in the oil, reserving 1 tablespoon, for 5-10 minutes until all of the water is released and they have fried to desired texture. Remove from pan.

    2. Add last tablespoon of oil into pan/pot, and saute ginger, garlic, white scallion parts, and chilis for 1 minute. Add in chinese wine and cook off and then add in chili bean paste and sichuan peppercorns and coat the aromatics in the paste, frying for another minute.

    3. Take a tablespoon or two of broth and mix in corn starch. Add in soy sauces and chicken broth to pan/pot and warm up. Once the sauce is simmering, add in mushrooms and then corn starch mixture.

    4. On a gentle simmer, gently add in tofu and cook through. Serve with rice, green scallions.
