    Pumpkin Waffles

    Ingredients

    Optional Maple Pecan Topping

    Directions

    1. Preheat waffle maker on medium-high heat. Preheat oven to 200°F (93°C) and place a wire rack on a baking sheet.

    2. In a large bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, ginger, and salt together until combined.

    3. In another large bowl, beat eggs on high speed for 1–2 minutes until frothy on top. Add pumpkin, melted butter, brown sugar, maple syrup, vanilla, and milk, and whisk or beat on medium speed until combined.

    4. Pour dry ingredients into wet ingredients and whisk or beat on low speed until combined (do not over-mix; some small lumps are okay).

    5. Grease preheated waffle maker, pour batter into wells, and cook until crisp (about 5–6 minutes). Transfer cooked waffles to the wire rack in the oven to keep warm while cooking remaining batches.

    6. For the optional maple pecan topping, place pecans in a saucepan over medium-low heat and toast for 4–5 minutes until fragrant. Turn heat to low, add maple syrup, warm through for a minute, and remove from heat to serve over waffles.
