    Moussaka

    Ingredients

    Eggplant & Potato Layer

    Meat Sauce

    Béchamel Sauce

    Directions

    1. Salt eggplant slices and set aside in a colander for 30 minutes to draw out moisture. Rinse and pat thoroughly dry with paper towels.

    2. Preheat oven to 400°F. Arrange eggplant and potato slices on oiled baking sheets, brush tops with olive oil, and season with salt. Roast until tender and lightly golden, about 20–25 minutes. Set aside and reduce oven temperature to 350°F.

    3. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant. Add ground lamb and cook, breaking up with a wooden spoon, until browned. Drain excess fat if needed.

    4. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute. Pour in red wine and simmer until mostly evaporated. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, cinnamon, allspice, bay leaf, salt, and pepper. Simmer over low heat until thick, about 20 minutes. Remove bay leaf.

    5. Meanwhile, melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook, stirring constantly, for 1–2 minutes until smooth and pale golden. Slowly pour in warmed milk while whisking constantly to prevent lumps. Simmer gently, whisking often, until thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5–7 minutes.

    6. Remove béchamel from heat and stir in nutmeg, salt, and grated cheese. Temper the beaten egg yolks with a splash of the warm sauce, then whisk them back into the saucepan until smooth.

    7. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish. Layer the roasted potatoes evenly on the bottom, followed by half of the eggplant slices. Spread the meat sauce evenly over the eggplant, then top with the remaining eggplant slices.

    8. Pour the béchamel sauce over the top, smoothing it into an even layer, and sprinkle with extra grated cheese.

    9. Bake at 350°F for 45–50 minutes until the top is puffed and deep golden brown. Let rest for 20–30 minutes before slicing and serving.
