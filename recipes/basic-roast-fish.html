    Basic Roast Fish

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 400F or 350F. The lower temperature will cook the fish slower, but will result in more succulent final product. If you have the time, the lower temperature is better.

    2. Look at fillet. If the cut has uneven thickness, consider cutting in half, so you can take the thinner half out when it is done. Decide if you are going to cook in a roasting pan or “papillote” (parchment paper or tin foil packet). In a packet, you will get less browning but hopefully more succulent fish. Season fish with olive oil, salt and pepper, and any seasoning rub. Place in cooking vessel and dress with any herbs and lemon slices and place any butter on top. Put any wine cooking vessel.

    3. Put in oven. Start checking temperature with an instant read thermometer in 10 minutes. 145F is medium. It should take 15 to 20 minutes to cook, but could take longer or shorter depending on oven and cut. If you want to get fancy, you can broil fish in a roasting pan to brown the top (1 to 2 minutes) and then finish in the oven at 400F. You should stay near the fish the entire time it is broiling, to ensure you don’t burn it. Serve with a simple vegetable side and, if desired, potatoes.

    Watercress Sauce for Fish

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Chop everything in a food processor. Slowly add olive oil, until you achieve the consistency you want. I prefer this to be chunky, instead of a more viscous green sauce. Check the seasoning, adding black pepper and lemon juice as you think fit, but no salt. Stir in hemp seeds when the sauce is removed from the food processor.
