    Pasta Carbonara

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil. In a small saucepan, combine olive oil and meat, and turn heat to medium. Cook, stirring occasionally, until nicely browned, about 10 minutes. In the last minute, add the minced garlic (if using.) Turn off heat.

    2. Salt water, and cook pasta until tender but not mushy. Reserve about a cup of water before draining pasta.

    3. While pasta is cooking, beat 3 eggs in a large warmed bowl (large enough to hold pasta). Stir in about 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan and the meat and its juices.

    4. When pasta is done, drain and toss with egg mixture in the large bowl. NEVER make carbonara in a pot or pan that has been on heat as the egg will cook and not emulsify properly into a sauce. If the dressed pasta is dry, add a little reserved cooking water. Add plenty of black pepper and more Parmesan to taste, and serve.
