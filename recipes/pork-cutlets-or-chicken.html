    Pork Cutlets (or chicken)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Put flour in rimmed plate (or plate or bowl), beat egg in another rimmed plate, and put a 50/50 mixture of panko and flour (the flour makes the panko adhere and fry more evenly) in rimmed plate, all in a row. Season flour, egg, and panko with salt and pepper (yes all three.) Keeping one hand dry, put each cutlet in flour, egg, and then panko and set aside on a plate.

    2. Heat a high-heat, neutral oil (I use safflower) over medium-high heat. Add cutlets and cook each side 3-5 minutes until coating is nicely browned on both sides and cooked through. Do not crowd the pan, you will almost certainly need to cook cutlets in batches.

    3. Serve with lemon juice and capers or the below beet puree. Either way, they are great with arugula.

    Chicken Variation

    Do everything same but replace the pork cutlets with chicken breasts. You can also add hot sauce to the egg for further seasoning. Otherwise, everything is the same.

    Beet Puree

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat olive oil in a small saucepan over medium-low. Cook shallot, stirring occasionally, until soft but without taking on any color, about 4 minutes. Add beet, apple, and broth; season with salt and pepper. Increase heat to medium and bring to a boil; cook until liquid is almost completely evaporated and beets are softened, 15–20 minutes. Let cool 10 minutes, then transfer to a blender. Add cream and yogurt and blend until sauce is the consistency of chunky apple sauce (no large pieces should remain). Season with salt and pepper.
