    Classic Sage and Sausage Stuffing

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Spread the bread cubes in a single layer on two large rimmed baking sheets. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, tossing halfway through, until dry and lightly toasted. Remove from oven and transfer to a very large mixing bowl.

    2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon into small pieces, until browned and cooked through, about 6 to 8 minutes. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the cooked sausage to the bowl with the toasted bread, leaving the rendered fat in the skillet.

    3. Add the butter to the skillet with the sausage fat over medium heat. Once melted, add the onions, celery, and carrots (if using). Season with salt and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are softened and the onions are translucent, about 8 to 10 minutes.

    4. Add the garlic, sage, thyme, and rosemary to the skillet. Cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Pour in the white wine to deglaze the pan, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom, and cook until the wine is mostly reduced, about 3 to 4 minutes.

    5. Pour the cooked vegetable and herb mixture over the bread and sausage in the large bowl. Toss everything together thoroughly.

    6. In a small bowl, whisk together 2 cups of chicken broth and the beaten eggs. Pour the mixture over the stuffing and toss gently until the bread is evenly moistened. (If the mixture still looks dry or the bread isn't sufficiently hydrated, add up to 1 more cup of broth). Taste a small amount of the bread mixture and season with additional salt and pepper as needed.

    7. Transfer the stuffing mixture to a greased 9x13-inch baking dish (or equivalent casserole dish). Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake for 30 minutes.

    8. Remove the foil and continue baking for another 20 to 25 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and crisp. Remove from the oven, let rest for 10 minutes, and serve warm.
