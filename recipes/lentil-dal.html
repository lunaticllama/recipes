    Lentil Dal

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Place the lentils in a pot and add 4 cups of water to cover them. Most recipes say to use a 3 to 1 ratio of water to lentils, but because the lentils will be added to a watery sauce to finish cooking, use a little bit less here. Bring the water to a boil, skimming off any scum that rises to the top, then turn down the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, or until creamy and tender.

    2. While the lentils are cooking, heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a heavy-based pan over medium-high heat. Add the onion and fry gently for about 5 minutes, until translucent and beginning to brown. Add the tomatoes and ginger, and cook for 3 minutes, until tomatoes soften. Add the turmeric and stir. Stir in the greens and let them wilt, time depends on the green.

    3. Stir in the onion-tomato-greens into the lentils or vice versa depending on which pot can better fit the dal, and simmer for a few minutes to warm through, the flavors meld, and everything is fully cooked-through. Add more water if the lentils need some to finish cooking. Season to taste with the salt.

    4. In a small pan, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil, and add the cumin seeds and garlic clove. Fry for 30 seconds, until the cumin sizzles, then turn off the heat and pour the seasoned oil, cumin seeds and garlic into the dal. Cover and let it infuse.

    5. Stir before serving, and serve hot with Naan or roti or other indian bread and a salad on the side.
