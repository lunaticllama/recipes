    Jerk Chicken

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Wash and thoroughly pat dry chicken thighs. Salt both sides of thighs and and put in fridge for several hours.

    2. Put together all ingredients other than the chicken in a mini-chop, food processor, or blender and puree.

    3. 2-4 hours before baking, put chicken thighs in a bowl and cover in marinade. Make sure to thoroughly cover each chicken thigh with marinade, massaging it in. Use gloves or thoroughly wash your hands before and after, because the habanero can cause an uncomfortable chili burn.

    4. Pre-heat oven to 350. Put a wire rack in a rimmed baking sheet and evenly place chicken thighs on rack (you can do it with the wire rack, but the chicken won’t be as crispy.) Bake until chicken thighs read 155-160 degrees fahrenheit with an instant read thermometer. If you have leftover marinade, I baste the chicken thighs with it every 10 or 15 minutes. Then, put move chicken to broiler and broil 3-5 minutes until skin is crispy and charred. Serve with rice and greens. Anything with pineapple (or just pineapple) would also make a great side.
