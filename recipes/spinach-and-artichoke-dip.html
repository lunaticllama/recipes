    Spinach and Artichoke Dip

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Pre-heat oven to 350F.

    2. Melt butter in saute pan over medium heat. When butter is melted, add garlic. Saute for 30 second and then add spinach. Toss spinach so that it wilts evenly. When spinach is nearly wilted, add frozen artichokes, and saute until unfrozen and soft. Pour spinach and artichoke mixture onto cutting board and finely chop as desired.

    3. Put cream cheese in microwave safe bowl and microwave until soft and creamy. Add spinach and artichoke mixture and rest of ingredients and thoroughly mix. Season with salt and pepper and place in baking dish. Bake for 30 minutes.
