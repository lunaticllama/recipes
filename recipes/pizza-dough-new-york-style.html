    Pizza Dough (New York Style)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Whisk together the water, yeast, olive oil, and salt. Let the yeast foam for 5 minutes.

    2. Add in 3 cups of flour and use your dough hook to knead. Knead for a minimum of 5 minutes, until the dough is smooth and just slightly tacky, but not sticky. Add the additional .5 cup of flour, if needed, and knead until smooth. I usually add the half cup of flour to get the tacky texture you want.

    3. Place the dough in a large, well oiled bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and put in a warm place to proof for at least 1 hour, or wrap the dough in plastic wrap and store in your refrigerator until you're ready to use it. If using the dough from the refrigerator, let it come to room temperature before working with it (rest for at least an hour.)

    4. Divide dough into two halves and let each dough ball rest for 20-30 minutes. Stretch, cover, and bake at hottest temperature your oven gets to on a pizza stone or steel!
