    General Tso’s Stir Fry

    Ingredients

    Sauce

    Tofu

    Stir Fry

    Directions

    1. Place all tofu ingredients besides tofu and cornstarch in bowl and mix to combine. Chop tofu into pieces and then place in zip lock bag with sauce. Shake contents to cover tofu and then begin adding cornstarch, 1 tablespoon at a time, until cornstarch and sauce form a coating that covers the tofu.

    2. Fry tofu in oil in a cast iron pan over medium heat, flipping to fry all sides until crispy. When you take tofu out of bag, let excess sauce pour off, before placing in hot pan.

    3. Reserve fried tofu as needed. In a clean pan, saute fried tofu, scallions, and chilis, and then while on heat toss in sauce and serve.
