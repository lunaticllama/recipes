    Directions

    1. Whisk eggs into milk and stir in butter, vanilla (if using), and yeast (if using).

    2. Whisk the dry ingredients together and then mix milk mixture with dry ingredients.

    3. Let the batter rest for at least 15 minutes, while the griddle is heating; it'll thicken slightly. Let rest overnight on countertop if making yeasted pancakes

    4. Cook 1/4 cupfuls of batter onto the lightly greased griddle. For blueberry pancakes, sprinkle the fresh or thawed frozen blueberries over the batter.
