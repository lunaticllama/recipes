    Eggplant Stuffed with Haloumi

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 400 F.. Carefully score the eggplant flesh without cutting into skin. Rub 2 tsp salt into flesh and set aside for 5-10 minutes, then rinse and pat dry. Combine cumin with 2 tbs olive oil and brush over flesh. Heat a large frypan over medium heat and cook eggplant, flesh-side down, for 10-15 minutes until dark golden. Transfer eggplant, flesh-side up, to a baking tray lined with baking paper. Bake for 30-40 minutes until a skewer easily pierces flesh. Cool slightly.

    2. Using a spoon, scoop out eggplant flesh, leaving 1cm attached and reserving skin. Combine eggplant mixture with pine nuts, garlic, basil leaves, sumac, half the cheese and remaining 1 tbs oil. Spoon into eggplant skin and top with remaining cheese. Bake for 15 minutes or until golden. Scatter with extra pine nuts and basil leaves to serve.
