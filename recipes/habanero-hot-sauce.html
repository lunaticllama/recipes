    Directions

    1. Cook unpeeled garlic in a saucepan on high heat, until browned and softened, 10-15 minutes. Take out of pan and peel when cool.

    2. Add vinegar, carrot, onion, and peppers to saucepan and cook on medium heat until carrot is softened, about 10 minutes. Add garlic cloves when they are peeled to vinegar mixture.

    3. Purée vinegar mixture in blender or food processor with sugar and salt. Add water to achieve desired consistency.
