    Aunt Mary’s Chicken Tenders

    Directions

    1. Preheat over to 350F. Put French’s Fried Onions and flour in plastic bag and beat until it looks like a breading mixture.

    2. Dip chicken into egg; then coat with onion crumbs on both sides, pressing firmly to adhere. Place chicken on baking sheet.

    3. Bake breaded chicken 20 to 25 minutes minutes or until golden brown and cooked through.
