    Pasta with Braised Lamb Shank Ragu

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Choose a stock pot, Dutch oven or braising dish that will hold the shanks and other ingredients so that the shanks can sit in one layer. Preheat oven to 325.

    2. Pat the shanks dry with paper towel and season with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil in the pot over medium heat and brown the shanks on all sides, about 10 minutes. When the shanks are browned, transfer them to a plate or platter.

    3. Pour out the used oil from the pot and replace with more olive oil. Toss the carrots, celery, and onions into the pot. Season with salt (about 1 tsp) and cook over low heat, stirring until the vegetables are softened but not coloured, about 10 to 15 minutes.

    4. Add in garlic and anchovies and saute while tossing for 1 to 2 minutes. Add in 1 ½ cup of white wine and deglaze. Reduce wine to ¾ cup of liquid.

    5. we open the can of tomatoes and pour everything into a bowl. Using your hands to crush the tomatoes into bite sized chunks. Add the tomatoes and their juices, the olives and the citrus peel (if using) and the pepper into the pot. Stir everything to combine and bring up to a boil.

    6. Add the lamb shanks to the pot. Ideally they should fit in one layer, and should be at least 3/4 submerged under the liquids. If not, add more white wine. Bring to a boil if lamb shanks or wine lowered the temperature of the liquid, place a lid on the pot, and then braise in oven. Check after half an hour to ensure there should be some bubbling going on. Adjust the heat to maintain this. Braise at least 2.5 hours and possible 3.5 hours until the lamb is tender and falling off the bone.

    7. Take braise out of oven and take out shanks and sprigs of herbs (and orange peel). Take meat off bone and shred and return to pot and bring to a slight simmer over stove if braise has cooled down. Serve over pasta of choice (fresh linguine is best) with chopped parsley.
