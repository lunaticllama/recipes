    Master Quiche Recipe

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Preheat oven to 425F and set rack to center position. Line the dough with parchment paper, allowing it to overhang the sides, and fill the pan with pie weights, dried beans, or dry rice. Bake the crust for 10 minutes. Remove weights and parchment paper, wrap edges of the pie dough with tin foil, and return to the oven. Bake until lightly golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes. Remove and lower oven temperature to 350F. If not using crust, skip this step and just preheat oven to 350F.

    2. In a large bowl, whisk cream and milk with eggs until thoroughly incorporated. Whisk salt and pepper into custard base.

    3. Scatter half of cheese and vegetable filling all over bottom of pie and set on a rimmed baking sheet. Pour in half of custard mixture or until first layer of fillings and cheese is covered. Add second half of cheese and vegetable filling and pour remaining custard on top. Arrange decorative herbs on top of custard.

    4. Bake until center is just barely set but still jiggles when touched, about 35 minutes. Remove from oven and allow to cool at least 15 minutes before serving. Serve warm, sprinkling minced chives on top, if desired.

    Note

    various vegetable filling ideas include (1) mushrooms/shallots or mushrooms/leeks with blue cheese; (2) spinach and leek with feta, (3) bacon/scallions/onions with gruyere or cheddar; (4) asparagus/shallot with fontina or gruyere (5) mushrooms/onions and swiss; (6) smoked salmon/red onion with swiss or gruyere.

    Note

    all vegetables or bacon should be fully cooked/sauteed before adding to quiche. Spinach and mushrooms need all of the water cooked out, otherwise they will destroy the consistency of your custard.
