    Braised Lamb Shanks (Pressure Cooker)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Season lamb shanks and then brown in a large saute pan on all sides in olive oil or other neutral oil.

    2. Reserve shanks, add a tablespoon of additional oil if necessary, then saute onion, carrots, celery, and fennel, if using. Once the vegetables are softened, add tomato paste and garlic and saute another minute. Deglaze with wine and bring to a boil.

    3. Add vegetables and wine to pressure cooker. Add lamb shanks and juices on top. Add in bay leaves, herbs, cinnamon and chiles (if using). Add vegemite and lemon rind.

    4. Seal pressure cooker and then cook on high pressure (12 psi) for 50 minutes. Once lamb is done, strain out the vegetables from the gravy and serve with gremolata on the side
