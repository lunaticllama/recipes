    Phil’s Cajun Pasta

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat olive in flat-sided saute pan over medium heat. When hot, add reserve shrimp shells and fry until turning golden. While shells cook, pat shrimp dry, and then toss with 2 tablespoons of cajun seasoning. Season with salt and pepper. Remove shells and then saute shrimp over medium heat in the flavored oil until blackened, about 1 minute each side. Remove shrimp and then add sausage and saute until browned. Once browned, remove. Reserve oil from shrimp and sausage.

    2. Bring a pot of water to boil and cook penne according to instructions.

    3. Add pepper, shallots, and scallions to saute pan with reserved oil. Add a tablespoon of water if necessary and allow liquid of vegetables to begin to deglaze pan. After 5-10 minutes, when vegetables are soft, add garlic and saute 30 seconds. Add cherry tomatoes and saute until warm. Then, deglaze with wine. When wine has almost evaporated add in cream and bring to a simmer. Season with remaining cajun seasoning.

    4. When cream is about to simmer add back in the shrimp and sausage. Bring to a simmer. When pasta is done cooking, reserve ¼ cup of pasta cooking liquid, drain pasta, and then add pasta and liquid to sauce. Add in asiago cheese. Toss together until everything is emulsified.
