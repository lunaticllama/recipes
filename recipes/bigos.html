    Bigos

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Toss beef with flour and season with salt and pepper. Brown in a dutch oven in neutral oil. Reserve beef and discard the oil.

    2. In the same dutch oven, saute onion with the bacon until softened and beginning to turn golden.

    3. Meanwhile pre-heat the oven to 325 and bring a pot of water to boil. Boil the cabbage and the sauerkraut for 20 minutes.

    4. Once the onion is softened, add in the beef, kielbasa and cooked cabbage and sauerkraut. Add in the prunes, mushrooms and thyme. Add in wine and simmer for five minutes, then add in the stock.

    5. Braise the beef in the dutch oven in the oven covered for 2-3 hours until completely tender. Take the lid off the last 30-60 minutes to reduce liquid and brown, stirring occasionally.
