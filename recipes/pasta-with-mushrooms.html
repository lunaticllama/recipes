    Pasta with Mushrooms

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. If using, heat ¼ cup oil in a large Dutch oven or other heavy pot over medium. Arrange prosciutto in a single layer in pot and cook, turning once or twice, until crisp, about 5 minutes. Transfer to paper towels to drain.

    2. Heat 2 tbs oil in same pot over high. Cook button mushrooms, tossing occasionally, until browned and tender, 5–8 minutes. Remove from pan and reduce heat to medium-low. Add shallots/onions and 1 tsp. thyme, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring often, until shallots are sweet, tender, and somewhat caramelized, about 15 minutes. Add porcini mushrooms and cook for 2 or 3 minutes. Then add stock and cooked button mushrooms and reduce heat to low. Bring to a simmer and cook until only a thin layer of stock coats bottom of pot, 5−7 minutes.

    3. Meanwhile, cook pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring occasionally, until very al dente, about 3 minutes less than package directions.

    4. Reserve at least 1 cup of pasta cooking liquid. Then drain pasta and add to pot with mushrooms and add the ½ to 1 cup pasta cooking liquid (depending on how much more your pasta needs to cook.) If using, crumble half of prosciutto into pot. Increase heat to medium, bring to a simmer, and cook, tossing constantly, until pasta is al dente and liquid is slightly thickened, about 2 minutes. Add cream and butter, return to a simmer, and cook, tossing, until pasta is coated, about 1 minute. Taste and season with salt if needed.
