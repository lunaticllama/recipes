    Basic Curry and Variations

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Combine garlic, ginger, chilies, half of lemon juice, and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt in a mortar and pestle or in the small work bowl of a food processor and pound or process until a fine paste is produced. Instead of that, you can also just finely mince everything together (this is what I usually do.) Set aside.

    2. Heat oil in a large saucepan or Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add onion all at once, along with baking soda, if using. Cook, stirring frequently, until onions start to leave a brown coating on bottom of pan, 3 to 4 minutes. Add 1 tablespoon water, scrape up browned bits from pan, and continue cooking. If you have time or the patience, repeat this process until onions are a deep brown, about 10 minutes total.

    3. Immediately add garlic/ginger/chili paste all at once and stir to combine. Add coriander, black pepper, turmeric, and 1 teaspoon garam masala. Stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add tomatoes and crush them using a whisk or potato masher. Add drained, rinsed chickpeas and cilantro, reserving a little cilantro for garnish. Add 1/2 cup water.

    4. Bring to a simmer, cover with lid slightly cracked, and reduce heat to maintain a gentle bubbling. Cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid has reduced into a thick stew and spices have melded, about 30 minutes.

    5. Stir in remaining garam masala and lemon juice. Season to taste with salt. Serve with rice and/or naan, sprinkling additional cilantro on top.

    Variations

    substitute for chickpeas (or in addition to chickpeas), roasted cauliflower/broccoli, pressed and baked tofu (see Tofu Stir Fry for technique), spinach, kale, swiss chard, shrimp or chicken (saute first to brown and so chicken can cook through in sauce).

    Coconut Milk Variation

    Ingredients

    Additional Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Don’t use any chilis with the garlic/ginger paste. In a dutch oven over medium heat, warm the oil. Add the onion/shallots, garlic, ginger and salt and saute until softened but not browned. About 5 minutes. Add the cumin, curry powder, turmeric, sugar and saute another minute. Add the peanut butter, coconut milk, fish sauce (if using), tomatoes, chili paste and coconut water or broth and stir to mix. Let everything simmer on low for 15 minutes.

    2. At this point, you can transfer sauce to a blender and run the blender to create a smooth sauce (and then return to pot.) I almost never do and it is fine. Stir in the lime juice. Taste for seasoning. It should be somewhat thick, but still thin enough that the vegetables will cook in it’s heat. Add more broth, coconut water, or plain water if needed.

    3. Cut the tofu into 1” cubes and prepare all your vegetables. Add carrots, shallots, zucchini, or greens to the pot first and cook for 5 minutes, then add the tofu, bell pepper, or mushrooms and cook another 10-15 minutes until all the vegetables are just tender but not mushy.
