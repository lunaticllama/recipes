    Lentil Soup (Phil’s Italian Lentil Soup)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Saute the carrots, celery, onion, and fennel in a dutch oven or other soup pot for 8 to 10 minutes until the onion is translucent and the vegetables are soft. Add the garlic and tomato paste and stir for 30 seconds. Add the diced tomato and saute for another 2 minutes.

    2. Add the lentils, broth, zest, and fresh herbs. Bring to a boil, then simmer until lentils are at desired doneness, typically 40 or 50 minutes.

    3. When lentils are done, take two ladleful of the vegetables (try to leave some of the broth in each scoop in the soup pot) and add to a blender. Blend until completely pureed. Add the puree back to the soup pot and stir. The soup may be too thick. If necessary, add more water by measuring water in bowl of blender to help get any remaining bits of puree. When at desired consistency, bring to simmer, season with salt and pepper, and serve.
