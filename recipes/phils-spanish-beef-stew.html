    Phil’s Spanish Beef Stew

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Heat a braising pot over medium heat and add 1 tablespoon olive oil. Season the beef with salt and pepper generously, toss with flour until evenly covered, and brown all around to sear in a pot. Remove the beef and set aside.

    2. Drain pot of fat, then add another tablespoon of olive oil and the onion, pepper, carrots, and celery. Saute for 5 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste into the pot and saute for 2 more minutes. Pour in vermouth, stir to get up any browned bits, and reduce.

    3. Once vermouth is reduced to less than half of its original liquid, add tomatoes, olives, marmite, soy sauce, one cup of red wine, and water. Bring to a simmer and then add back in beef along with any collected juices. If the wine from first cup along with the water is not enough to almost cover the beef, then continue to add wine until the beef is almost covered. Season with salt and pepper.

    4. Cover and braise in the oven at 350F for 1.5 hour. After the first hour and a half, remove cover and make sure some of the beef is sticking above liquid. Continue to braise, turning beef over every half hour, so need sides are allowed to roast above the liquid. If liquid reduces too quickly, add more water or wine as desired.

    5. When beef is tender, remove from oven, and strain the sauce through fat separator. Return vegetables minus any herbs to pot with beef with strained sauce.
