    Zucchini Fritters (vegan or not)

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Shred the zucchini and onion on the large holes of a box grater or in a food processor with the shredding disk. Alternatively, finely dice the onion in lieu of shredding. Place the shredded vegetables in a colander in the sink and sprinkle with the salt. Toss to combine. Let drain for 30 minutes, then pick up by the handful and squeeze out as much moisture as possible. Place on a kitchen towel or double layer of paper towels.

    2. In a medium bowl, combine the eggs, garlic, cheese, herbs, lemon zest, and pepper. Beat well with a fork. Add the drained zucchini mixture and mix together. Sprinkle the 2 tablespoons of flour and baking powder on top and mix with a fork just until well combined. Keep on adding flour, 1 tablespoon at the time until desired consistency is achieved.

    3. Heat one tablespoon of the olive oil over medium-high heat in a wide, heavy pan. When the oil is hot, drop the batter into the pan by heaping tablespoonful. Cook for about three minutes on the first side, until nicely browned. Flip and cook for about two minutes more. Place the cooked pancakes on a paper towel-lined plate and repeat with the remaining oil and batter. Serve with Greek yogurt, sour cream, tzatziki or soy sauce.

    4. If making vegan, the fritters will be difficult to maneuver in a skillet. Instead of frying, you can placed fritters on a baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes in 400F oven, flipping them once midway through. They are ready when both sides are nicely browned and they are cooked through.

    5. Serve fritters with a simple greens salad and sauce, if desired.
