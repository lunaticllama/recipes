    Kale Caesar Salad

    Ingredients

    Directions

    1. Break down kale from thick stalks and chop to manageable size, wash, and reserve.

    2. First, make a fresh mayonnaise. Whisk the egg in small bowl, until yolk is emulsified with whites. Add some salt and whisk some more (salt further breaks up the egg.) Then slowly pour in oil in a steady stream while continuing to whisk, until an emulsion forms. If you add the oil too fast, the emulsion will break and require you to whisk an egg yolk into the mixture to reform the emulsion. Keep on adding oil, until you have about ½ or ⅔ of a cup (depending on how much kale you have) of the mayonnaise.

    3. Add anchovies, garlic, most of the cheese (can reserve 1 or 2 tablespoons), Worcestershire, lemon juice, mustard, and onion (if using). Whisk until dressing has ingredients evenly incorporated. Add lots of freshly cracked pepper.

    4. Coat kale with reserved cheese (this helps the dressing adhere to the kale.) Toss with dressing and serve.

    Optional protein addition

    Drain 1 can of chickpeas and rinse thoroughly. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil over medium-high heat in a cast iron pan and add chickpeas. Saute/fry until chickpeas are nice and brown, approximately 8-10 minutes. Serve chickpeas on top of kale salad.
